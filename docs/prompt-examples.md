# Prompt examples for Happy Horse

These prompts are written for testing the Happy Horse API through APIDot. They are starting points for product teams, not guaranteed outputs. Adjust brand names, subject details, source URLs, and safety rules before production use.

## 1. Text-to-video product teaser

A compact electric city bike gliding through a bright morning street market, smooth tracking shot, realistic reflections, lively background motion.

## 2. Image-to-video animation

Animate this product photo with a slow push-in, subtle studio reflections, clean lighting, and gentle background movement.

## 3. Reference-to-video scene

Character1 hands a glowing map to character2 in a neon train station, cinematic close-up then wide shot, preserve each reference character's look.

## 4. Video edit

Replace the source video background with a clean futuristic showroom while preserving camera motion, product shape, and subject timing.

## 5. Storyboard preview

A low-angle shot of a delivery robot crossing a rainy sidewalk, reflections on pavement, smooth cinematic tracking, social ad concept.

## 6. Reference style transfer

Use the reference image for art direction and color palette, create a short product reveal with matching mood, camera pacing, and lighting.

## Production prompt notes

- Store prompt versions together with task_id, model, user/job owner, and request payload.
- Keep source media URLs reachable long enough for APIDot to fetch them.
- Be explicit about camera motion, subject continuity, scene pacing, audio intent, duration, resolution, and aspect ratio.
- Do not log prompts or source media URLs if they may contain private customer data.
